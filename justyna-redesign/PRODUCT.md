# Cute Cut, Justyna Mahboob

## Register

brand

## Purpose

An artist portfolio for Justyna Mahboob. The work must be the strongest visual voice on every screen. Visitors should quickly understand who Justyna is, browse the complete collage archive by subject, open individual works, and make contact.

## Audience

- curators, publishers and cultural institutions;
- parents and educators interested in art workshops;
- people discovering Justyna through a shared work or search result;
- friends, collaborators and collectors returning to the archive.

## Existing content to preserve

- Home, Gallery and Contact navigation;
- the existing biography and contact address, available in Polish and English;
- the original Cute Cut opening composition, logo and Justyna's signature;
- all 171 archived media items;
- gallery groups: Food, Fruits & vegetables, Meat, Drinks, Cakes and Things;
- direct, distraction-free viewing of an individual work.

## Brand voice

Tactile, playful and exact. The interface should feel cut, placed and composed by hand, while remaining calm enough to let the collages lead. The handmade red Cute Cut mark is the canonical name and logo. Avoid childish illustration clichés, generic gallery minimalism and decorative portfolio copy.

## Safety boundary

All exploration and implementation happens in the local `justyna-redesign` directory. Nothing is uploaded to Cyberfolks until a direction is selected, finished and explicitly approved.
